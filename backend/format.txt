  <h2>Cost Estimation</h2>
  <p>Note that these are rough estimates, and actual costs can vary widely.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><b>Land Price:</b> Land prices in Una vary. Residential plots can range from ₹312/sq.ft to ₹4,958/sq.ft. Agricultural land may have different rates. For industrial purposes, land acquisition costs would need specific assessment.</li>
    <li><b>Refinery Setup (Detailed Breakdown):</b>
      <ul>
        <li>Electrolyzer costs: A 100 MW plant could cost around $245 million (USD 2,450 per kilowatt).</li>
        <li>Balance of plant: This includes rectifiers, transformers, cooling, and safety systems, adding 30-60% to electrolyzer hardware costs.</li>
        <li>Water treatment: Costs depend on the water source. Desalination, if required, adds to the cost.</li>
        <li>Compression and storage: Compression consumes 2-7 kWh per kg of hydrogen. Storage options range from high-pressure tanks to underground caverns.</li>
        <li>Site preparation, engineering, and permitting: These indirect costs and contingencies can significantly impact the initial capital.</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li><b>Pipelines:</b> Pipeline construction costs vary. A Gujarat gas pipeline project cost Rs 650 crore (approximately $760 million USD) for 18 million standard cubic meters per day capacity. Costs depend on diameter, material, and terrain.</li>
    <li><b>Labour:</b> Labour costs in Gujarat were reported at INR 1,255.52 per manday worked for all employees in March 2023. Minimum wages range from ₹12,649 to ₹13,507 per month depending on zone and skill level.</li>
    <li><b>Transportation:</b> Costs depend on the mode (pipeline, truck, ship). Transporting ammonia from Gujarat to Japan has been estimated.</li>
    <li><b>Raw Materials:</b> Raw materials include water and electricity. Green hydrogen production relies on renewable energy sources like solar and wind.</li>
    <li><b>Time Estimation (Setup Duration):</b> Plant construction can take several months to years. Kandla's green hydrogen plant was constructed in four months. Reliance is scaling its electrolyzer gigafactory to 3GW by 2026.</li>
  </ul>
