Green Hydrogen Blending Project Blending green hydrogen with natural gas. NTPC Kawas plant in Gujarat. Pilot project. Blending of 2.2-2.3% green hydrogen into natural gas supplied to over 4,000 residential and commercial customers in Ahmedabad, Gujarat [12].

Cost Estimation (3 kWh Capacity)

Note that these are rough estimates, and actual costs can vary widely. The costs provided are scaled down to a 3 kWh capacity unless stated otherwise.

  • Land Price:
    • Industrial land prices in Surat vary significantly. Sachin, Pandesara and Udhna are key industrial areas [4, 6, 8].
    • Examples:
    • Pandesara: ₹4.21 Cr for 9423 sq. ft [8].
    • Udhna: ₹2.31 Cr for 5157 sq. ft [8].
    • Sachin: ₹4 Cr for 11023 sq. ft [4].
    For a small 3 kWh plant, land requirements would be minimal, potentially leasing space within an existing industrial facility could be more cost-effective.
  • Refinery Setup (Detailed Breakdown):
    • Electrolyzer: For a 3kW system, estimate ranges can vary widely, but a rough estimate would be $7,350 (scaling down from $2,450 per kilowatt for a 100 MW plant) [11].
    • Balance of Plant: Assume 30-60% of electrolyzer cost for rectifiers, transformers, cooling, and safety systems.
    • Water Treatment: Depends on the water source; include costs for purification.
    • Compression & Storage: For a small scale, consider smaller high-pressure tanks.
    • Site Preparation, Engineering, and Permitting: This can significantly impact costs.
  • Pipelines: For a 3 kWh plant integrated into an existing facility, pipeline costs would primarily involve internal connections. A major pipeline project in Surat cost Rs 4,841 crore [5]. Smaller pipeline construction projects in Surat cost approximately ₹36,45,856.15 [9].
  • Labour:
    • Labour costs in Gujarat were reported at INR 1,255.52 per manday worked [33]. Minimum wages range from ₹12,649 to ₹13,507 per month depending on zone and skill level [33].
    • For a small plant, operational labor costs would be relatively low, primarily for monitoring and maintenance.
  • Transportation: Transportation costs for a 3 kWh plant are minimal as the hydrogen produced will likely be used on-site.
  • Raw Materials: Raw materials include water and electricity. Green hydrogen production relies on renewable energy sources like solar and wind [18, 19].
  • Time Estimation (Setup Duration): A small-scale 3 kWh plant could be set up and commissioned within a few months.
