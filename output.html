Cost Estimation

Note that these are rough estimates, and actual costs can vary widely.

  • Land Price: Industrial land prices in Delhi vary significantly by location.
    • Mohan Cooperative Industrial Estate: ₹ 27 Cr for 17433 sq.ft [10, 15].
    • Rama Road, Kirti Nagar: ₹ 20 Cr for 5994 sq.ft [10].
    • Okhla Industrial Area: ₹ 21 Cr for 5499 sq.ft [15].
  • Refinery Setup (Detailed Breakdown): Costs can vary widely based on the size, technology and complexity.
    • A large oil refinery (60 million tonnes a year) can cost around $30 billion (₹2 lakh crore) [6, 16].
    • Mpower Refineries offers small to medium sized Petroleum Refinery projects on Turn-key basis with its own indigeneously developed technology, with capacities varying from 10000BPD to 60000BPD [23].
  • Pipelines: Costs depend on diameter, material, and terrain.
  • Labour: Minimum wages in Delhi are revised every six months [7].
    • For unskilled laborers: Approximately Rs 18,066 per month [3, 4].
    • For semi-skilled workers: Approximately Rs 19,929 per month [3, 4].
    • For skilled workers: Approximately Rs 21,917 per month [3, 4].
  • Transportation: Costs depend on the mode (pipeline, truck, ship).
  • Raw Materials: Raw materials include water and electricity. Green hydrogen production relies on renewable energy sources like solar and wind.
  • Time Estimation (Setup Duration): Setting up a refinery or hydrogen production plant can take several months to years.
